Top 10 Common Pool Care Mistakes Perth Pool Owners Make And How to Avoid Them

Owning a swimming pool in Perth is a luxury—but only if it’s maintained properly. Many homeowners unknowingly make mistakes that lead to cloudy water, algae, and costly repairs. Let’s explore the most common pool cleaning mistakes and how to avoid them.

Mistake #1 – Ignoring Water Chemistry

Unbalanced water is the #1 reason people ask, “why is my pool cloudy?”

Solution: Test water weekly and maintain correct pH and chlorine levels.

Mistake #2 – Inconsistent Cleaning Schedule

Skipping regular cleaning causes debris and algae buildup.

Solution: Follow consistent pool care tips Perth professionals recommend.

Mistake #3 – Overusing Pool Chemicals

More chemicals don’t mean cleaner water.

Solution: Use correct dosages to avoid skin irritation and equipment damage.

Mistake #4 – Not Cleaning Pool Filters

Dirty filters reduce circulation and water clarity.

Solution: Clean or backwash filters as recommended.

Mistake #5 – Running the Pool Pump Incorrectly

Running the pump too little or too much increases costs.

Solution: Adjust pump run-time seasonally.

Mistake #6 – Skipping Professional Inspections

Small issues often go unnoticed without expert checks.

Solution: Schedule routine professional pool cleaning Perth inspections.

Mistake #7 – Poor Algae Prevention

Once algae spreads, it’s harder to remove.

Solution: Use preventative treatments and proper circulation.

Mistake #8 – Ignoring Seasonal Changes

Perth’s climate changes affect pool care needs.

Solution: Adjust maintenance routines seasonally.

Mistake #9 – DIY Repairs Without Experience

Incorrect DIY fixes can worsen problems.

Solution: Leave technical repairs to professionals.

Mistake #10 – Waiting Too Long to Seek Help

Delays increase damage and costs.

Solution: Act early and consult pool experts.
