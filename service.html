Our Services

We provide a complete range of professional pool services including equipment supply, installation, maintenance, repairs, heating solutions, and cleaning. Our expert team is committed to delivering reliable, efficient, and high-quality solutions tailored to your pool’s needs.

Pool Equipment Supply

We supply and replace major brand pumps, filters, and chlorination systems with high standard installation. Our solutions ensure efficient, reliable performance for long-term pool operation.

Electric Pool Heat Pumps

Our energy-efficient heating solutions deliver year-round pool comfort, tailored to Perth’s climate, ensuring consistent, reliable performance while keeping operating costs low and energy usage minimal.

Pool Equipment Relocation

We provide professional pool equipment relocation with safe disconnection and careful repositioning, ideal for renovations or improved aesthetics, ensuring precise reinstallation.

Pool Covers & Roller Systems

We supply premium pool covers and roller systems that reduce evaporation, retain heat, lower maintenance costs, and protect your pool investment, designed for WA conditions.

Pool Troubleshooting & Leak Detection

We offer expert pool troubleshooting, accurate leak detection, and systematic problem identification, delivering efficient solutions that save time and costs while ensuring long-term reliability.

Pool Light Conversions to LED

Our modern LED lighting upgrades deliver enhanced brightness and improved safety while using less power. The result is an energy-efficient solution with a clean, elegant finish that elevates the overall look.

Green Pool Treatment Specialists

We restore green and neglected pools using professional-grade treatments to rebalance water, eliminate algae, and deliver fast, effective results with crystal-clear water you can see immediately

Pool Equipment Inspections

We perform a detailed pool inspection to identify potential repair costs early and ensure compliance with required standards. This gives you complete peace of mind before purchase by clearly assessing the pool’s condition.

Professional Pool Cleaning Services

Professional Pool Cleaning Services ensure clean, safe, and hygienic pools through regular cleaning, water treatment, and expert maintenance for long lasting performance.

At 6 Star Pools Perth, we are dedicated to delivering professional, reliable, and high quality pool services that cater for residential clients across Perth. Contact us today for a free phone consultation or quote.
