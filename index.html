Our Services

Electric Pool Heat Pumps

Our energy-efficient heating solutions provide year-round pool comfort, tailored specifically to Perth’s climate. Enjoy consistent, reliable heating with minimal operating costs.

Pool Equipment Relocation

We offer professional relocation of pool equipment with safe disconnection and careful repositioning, ideal for renovations or improved aesthetics. Our precise reinstallation ensures seamless and reliable operation.

Pool Covers & Roller Systems

We supply premium pool covers and roller systems that reduce evaporation and retain heat, helping lower maintenance costs. Designed for WA conditions, they protect and extend the life of your pool investment.

Pool Equipment Supply & Replacement

We supply and replace major brand pumps, filters, and chlorination systems with high-standard installation. Our solutions ensure efficient, reliable performance for long-term pool operation.

Pool Light Conversions to LED

Our modern LED lighting upgrades deliver enhanced brightness and improved safety while using less power. The result is an energy-efficient solution with a clean, elegant finish that elevates the overall look.

Pool Troubleshooting & Leak Detection

We provide expert troubleshooting for all pool-related issues, offering accurate leak detection and systematic problem identification. Our efficient solutions are designed to save you both time and costs while ensuring long-term reliability.

Green Pool Treatment Specialists

We restore green and neglected pools using professional-grade treatments to rebalance water and eliminate algae. Our fast and effective process delivers crystal-clear results you can see immediately.

Pool Equipment Inspections

We perform a detailed pool inspection to identify potential repair costs early and ensure compliance with required standards. This gives you complete peace of mind before purchase by clearly assessing the pool’s condition.

At 6 Star Pools Perth, we are dedicated to delivering professional, reliable, and high quality pool services that cater for residential clients across Perth. Contact us today for a free phone consultation or quote.
